STUDENT GUIDANCE

How VELORA HELP works for students

Students don't always know which opportunity is suitable. Our model is to understand the student's requirement first and then provide direction.

01

Tell us your goal

Tell us whether you need a scholarship, internship, course, admission, job or other help.

02

Share your details

Tell us your education level, course, location and requirement.

03

We review

VELORA HELP reviews the request and identifies relevant available information.

04

Get direction

We explain the next step, documents and where the student should apply or enquire.

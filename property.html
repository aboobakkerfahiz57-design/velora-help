FOR OWNERS & PROPERTY AGENTS

Have a property to list?

Owners, brokers and real-estate professionals can submit property information to VELORA HELP. We can review the listing before publishing it on the platform.

🏠 Property Listing Submit property type, location, rent/sale information and description.
📸 Photos Property photos can be submitted for review before publication.
🤝 Customer Enquiries Customer enquiries are routed through VELORA HELP rather than exposing your personal number publicly.
PROPERTY SUBMISSION

Submit your property

Send the property details to VELORA HELP. The current website is free to submit. Partnership plans can be introduced later when the platform is established.

🔐 Important: Do not enter OTPs, passwords, bank PINs or other sensitive security information. Property ownership and legal documents should be verified appropriately before any transaction.

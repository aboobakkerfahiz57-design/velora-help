VELORA HELP • PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This page explains the types of information VELORA HELP may receive when you use the website, contact the helpdesk or submit an enquiry, and how that information is intended to be handled.

On this page

1. Introduction 2. Information We Receive 3. How Information Is Used 4. WhatsApp Enquiries 5. Information Sharing 6. Healthcare Information 7. Third-Party Services 8. Security 9. Data Retention 10. Children 11. Your Choices 12. Policy Changes 13. Contact Us

1. Introduction

VELORA HELP, powered by VELORA DIGITAL, is a people-focused help and direction platform intended to connect people with useful information, services, opportunities and appropriate next steps.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the VELORA HELP website or submit an enquiry through the website.

By using the website, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. If you do not agree with the way information is described here, please do not submit personal information through the website.

2. Information We Receive

Depending on the feature you use, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Phone number.
  • Email address.
  • Taluk, town, village or general location.
  • The category of your enquiry.
  • Details about the problem or requirement.
  • Your preferred method of contact.
  • Information you voluntarily include in a WhatsApp enquiry.

Please provide only information that is reasonably necessary for your enquiry.

Important:
Do not submit passwords, OTPs, banking PINs, card numbers, account passwords or other security credentials through the VELORA HELP enquiry system.

3. How Information Is Used

Information submitted to VELORA HELP may be used to:

  • Understand your enquiry or problem.
  • Contact you regarding your request.
  • Provide information or direction relevant to your request.
  • Identify potentially relevant services, organisations, opportunities or official channels.
  • Follow up on suitable enquiries.
  • Improve the website and helpdesk process.
  • Understand recurring local problems and service needs.

The purpose is to help understand your requirement and provide useful direction. Submission of an enquiry does not guarantee that a particular organisation or authority will take action.

4. WhatsApp Enquiries

The current VELORA HELP website may prepare an enquiry message and open WhatsApp using the VELORA HELP contact number.

When you choose to send that message, the information is transmitted through WhatsApp and is subject to the privacy and security practices of WhatsApp and your device.

VELORA HELP does not control the operation, security, availability or privacy practices of third-party messaging services.

5. Information Sharing

VELORA HELP aims to limit unnecessary sharing of personal information.

Where appropriate, information may need to be communicated to a relevant person, service provider, organisation or authority in order to respond to a particular enquiry.

For example, if a person asks for direction regarding a local service, the relevant details may be used to help identify an appropriate service.

Information should not be described as being shared with a particular organisation unless that sharing is actually required or carried out.

VELORA HELP does not intend to sell personal enquiry information as a general business practice.

6. Healthcare Information

Healthcare enquiries require additional care.

VELORA HELP may receive information when a person asks for hospital, doctor, appointment or healthcare direction.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary medical records, diagnostic reports, prescriptions, passwords, insurance credentials or other sensitive information through a general website enquiry.

Information provided through VELORA HELP should not be considered a medical diagnosis or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

In an emergency, users should contact the appropriate emergency medical service or hospital directly.

7. Third-Party Services

The website may contain links or connections to third-party websites and services, including official government websites, hospitals, colleges, businesses, maps, WhatsApp or other external platforms.

Once you leave the VELORA HELP website and use an external service, that service's own terms and privacy policy may apply.

VELORA HELP is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites that it does not control.

8. Security

Reasonable efforts should be made to protect information handled by the VELORA HELP team.

However, no website, device, communication channel or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should therefore avoid submitting unnecessary highly sensitive information through ordinary enquiry channels.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain appropriate business records, improve services, resolve disputes, meet applicable legal obligations or operate the platform.

As the VELORA HELP platform develops, its data-management procedures may be updated to include more structured enquiry records, status tracking and deletion processes.

10. Children

The website may contain information useful to students and young people.

Children and minors should not submit unnecessary personal information. Where a service requires parental or guardian involvement, users should follow the requirements of the relevant institution or service.

11. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may contact VELORA HELP regarding information you have submitted through the platform.

You may also choose not to provide optional information. However, some information may be necessary for the team to understand or respond to an enquiry.

If you believe information submitted by you is incorrect, you can contact the helpdesk and request clarification or correction where reasonably possible.

12. Policy Changes

VELORA HELP may update this Privacy Policy as the website, services, technology, team structure and legal requirements develop.

The latest version published on this page should be treated as the current version for the website.

When major changes are made, the policy may be updated with a new effective date.

13. Contact VELORA HELP

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or about information you submitted through VELORA HELP, contact the helpdesk.

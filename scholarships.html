WHO MAY BE ELIGIBLE?

Eligibility depends on the scholarship.

Different scholarships can have different requirements. VELORA HELP can help you understand what information to look for before you apply.

🎓 Education

Your current class, course, year of study and institution may be relevant.

📊 Academic Performance

Some scholarships use marks, grades, rank or other academic criteria.

💰 Family / Financial Criteria

Some programmes may have income or financial eligibility requirements.

📍 Location

Residence, domicile or the location of the educational institution may matter for certain schemes.

🪪 Category / Eligibility

Some schemes have specific eligibility categories or requirements. Use the official scheme information to confirm eligibility.

📅 Deadline

Application deadlines can change. Always verify the current deadline before submitting an application.

COMMON DOCUMENTS

Keep your documents ready.

The exact documents vary by scholarship. Never upload documents to an unknown website or share sensitive credentials.

🪪

Identity Document

An accepted identity document may be required.

📜

Marks / Certificates

Previous examination marksheets or education certificates may be required.

🏦

Bank Information

Some scholarships require bank-account information for benefit transfer.

📄

Income Certificate

Certain need-based schemes may require an income certificate.

🏠

Residence / Domicile

Some schemes may require proof of residence or domicile.

📸

Photograph

A recent photograph may be required by the application portal.

🏫

College / School Details

Institution information and admission details may be needed.

📑

Other Certificates

Additional certificates may be required depending on the scheme.

🔐 Protect your information

VELORA HELP should never ask you to provide your OTP, password, UPI PIN, ATM PIN or banking password. Only provide documents through an official and trusted application process when they are actually required. Always verify scholarship eligibility, deadlines and application instructions through the official scholarship portal or responsible government/institutional authority.

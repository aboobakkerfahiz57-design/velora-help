EVERY REPORT SHOULD ANSWER

Five simple questions.

01 — What is the problem?

Explain exactly what is happening.

02 — Where did it happen?

Give the town, village, area, road or landmark.

03 — How often?

Tell us whether it happens once, occasionally, weekly, daily or continuously.

04 — How does it affect you?

Explain how the issue affects you, your family or the community.

05 — What solution would you like?

Tell us what you think should be done to solve the issue.

📊 Why collect this information?

Structured reports can help VELORA HELP identify repeated local problems and direct enquiries to the appropriate authority or organisation.

🚨 Emergency?

Do not use this report form for an immediate emergency. If there is an immediate threat to life, serious injury, fire, crime or another urgent danger, contact the appropriate emergency service immediately.

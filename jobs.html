JOB SAFETY

Find work safely.

A job platform must protect job seekers from misleading vacancies and recruitment scams.

🔎

Verify Employer

Employer details should be checked before a vacancy is presented as verified.

💰

Never Pay Suspicious Fees

Be cautious if someone demands money for a job, interview, appointment or guaranteed employment.

🛡️

Protect Your Information

Never share OTPs, passwords, banking PINs or other security credentials with a recruiter.

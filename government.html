KARNATAKA

Major welfare schemes

A simple starting point for understanding major Karnataka government programmes. Always verify current eligibility and application requirements through the official source before applying.

👩
WOMEN / FAMILY

Gruha Lakshmi

A Karnataka welfare programme focused on financial support for eligible women identified as heads of households.

Who can apply?
Eligibility depends on the current government rules and beneficiary criteria.
Documents
Identity, household and bank-related documents may be required.
Where?
Use the official Karnataka service platform or designated government channel.
Official Service
⚡
ELECTRICITY

Gruha Jyothi

A Karnataka household electricity support programme associated with free electricity up to the applicable monthly limit under the scheme rules.

Who can apply?
Eligible residential electricity consumers subject to current rules.
Documents
Electricity account details and identity information may be required.
Where?
Apply/check through the official Karnataka electricity/service channels.
Official Service
🧑‍🎓
YOUTH

Yuva Nidhi

A Karnataka programme providing financial assistance to eligible unemployed graduates and diploma holders while they seek opportunities or pursue development.

Who?
Eligible unemployed graduates and diploma holders under the current rules.
Documents
Education, identity, bank and employment-related information may be required.
Guidance
Verify current eligibility and application requirements before applying.
Official Service
🚌
TRANSPORT

Shakti Scheme

A Karnataka public transport welfare initiative providing eligible beneficiaries access to free travel on applicable state-run bus services.

Who?
Eligibility is based on the current government rules and domicile requirements.
Documents
Appropriate identity and domicile documentation may be required.
Where?
Check the applicable state transport corporation guidance.
Official Service
🍚
FOOD / WELFARE

Anna Bhagya

Karnataka food-security support for eligible beneficiaries through the public distribution and welfare system.

Who?
Eligible beneficiaries under the applicable food and ration rules.
Documents
Ration card and identity-related information may be required.
Where?
Use the official food department and Karnataka service channels.
Official Service

HOW IT WORKS

From problem to direction.

01 — Tell us the problem

Explain what happened, where it happened and how it is affecting people.

02 — VELORA HELP receives it

The enquiry can be sent to the VELORA HELP helpdesk through WhatsApp.

03 — Understand the responsible authority

The team can identify the relevant local authority or department where possible.

04 — Follow up

Where appropriate, VELORA HELP can guide the person on the next step and follow-up process.

Important information

VELORA HELP is an information, direction and enquiry platform. Submitting a report does not automatically mean that a government department or authority has officially received a complaint. For urgent danger, medical emergencies, fire, crime or other immediate emergencies, contact the appropriate emergency service directly. Do not submit highly sensitive personal information through a public problem report.

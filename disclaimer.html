1. General Disclaimer

VELORA HELP is a people-focused information and direction platform powered by VELORA DIGITAL.

The purpose of the platform is to help people understand where they may find useful services, information, opportunities or appropriate next steps.

Information displayed on the website is provided for general informational and directional purposes.

VELORA HELP does not guarantee that every piece of information is complete, current, available or suitable for every individual situation.

2. Direction Is Not a Guarantee

When VELORA HELP suggests a hospital, doctor, college, course, government service, job, property, business, worker or other service, the suggestion should be considered a direction rather than a guarantee.

The user should independently verify important details before making a decision.

Our goal:
VELORA HELP aims to make it easier to understand where to start. It does not promise a specific outcome from the organisation or service to which a user is directed.

3. Healthcare Disclaimer

Healthcare information available through VELORA HELP is provided for general information and direction only.

VELORA HELP is not a hospital, clinic, doctor, medical practice, emergency medical service or healthcare professional.

Any information about doctors, hospitals, departments, specialties, appointments or healthcare facilities should be independently confirmed.

A listing or recommendation does not constitute a medical diagnosis, prescription or professional medical advice.

Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional for their individual medical needs.

🚨 Medical Emergency

If you or another person is experiencing a medical emergency, severe symptoms, serious injury or immediate danger, do not wait for a response from VELORA HELP.

Contact the appropriate emergency service, hospital or qualified healthcare professional directly.

VELORA HELP may provide information about PUC admissions, degree admissions, colleges, courses, scholarships, entrance processes and educational opportunities.

6. Jobs, Internships and Opportunities

Job, internship, scholarship, course and opportunity information may be provided by external organisations, partners or public sources.

Users should verify:

  • The identity of the organisation.
  • The authenticity of the opportunity.
  • Eligibility requirements.
  • Application deadlines.
  • Fees or other charges.
  • Employment conditions.

VELORA HELP does not guarantee employment, internship selection, scholarship approval or admission.

⚠️ Be careful with fraud.

Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, card PIN, password or banking credentials with someone claiming to arrange a job, scholarship, admission or other service.

Property and rental listings may be provided by owners, brokers, agents, businesses or other third parties.

VELORA HELP does not automatically guarantee:

  • Ownership of a property.
  • Accuracy of property documents.
  • Property availability.
  • Rental terms.
  • Property price.
  • Broker identity.
  • Legal status of the property.

Users should conduct appropriate property, legal and financial verification before entering into an agreement or transferring money.

VELORA HELP does not automatically guarantee the quality, safety, conduct or performance of every service provider.

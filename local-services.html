HOW LOCAL SERVICE HELP WORKS

Tell us what you need.

01 — Select your service

Choose the type of worker or technician you need.

02 — Tell us your location

Give your town, village, area or locality in Dakshina Kannada.

03 — Send your enquiry

04 — Get direction

The VELORA HELP team can review the requirement and provide available local-service direction.

TRUST & VERIFICATION

Local workers should be verified before recommendation.

VELORA HELP can build a local network of service providers. Provider information should be checked before being shown as verified.

🪪 Identity

Where appropriate, collect and verify provider identity information before marking a provider as verified.

📞 Contact

Keep the provider's correct contact information in the VELORA HELP records.

📍 Service Area

Record the towns, villages and areas where the provider is willing to work.

⭐ Customer Feedback

Future versions can collect customer feedback and service history to improve recommendations.

🔧 Are you a local service provider?

If you are a plumber, electrician, technician, mechanic, carpenter, painter, cleaner or another local professional, you can contact VELORA HELP about joining the local service network.

Initially, provider registration can be handled through the VELORA HELP helpdesk. Future versions can introduce formal provider accounts, verification, service areas, plans and lead management.

Important safety information

VELORA HELP is a local information and direction platform. A provider should not be described as "verified" unless VELORA HELP has actually completed the relevant verification. Before hiring any worker, confirm the person's identity, service details, price, availability and work terms. Do not send advance payments to unknown providers merely because they appear on a website. For urgent electrical, gas, fire, medical or other dangerous situations, contact the appropriate emergency service.

OUR DIRECTION MODEL

What happens after you contact us?

The initial VELORA HELP model is simple: understand the problem, identify the relevant direction, communicate with the requester and, where appropriate, help connect them with the relevant service or authority.

01

You report

Tell us what happened, where it happened and what kind of help you need.

02

We understand

Our team reviews the information and contacts you when additional details are needed.

03

We guide

We identify the appropriate hospital, service, office, authority, organisation or next step where possible.

04

Follow-up

For suitable cases, the team can follow up and record the outcome.

PUBLIC PROBLEM ESCALATION

Need to raise a local issue?

For suitable public-interest problems, VELORA HELP can help organise the information so that the issue can be directed toward the appropriate local authority, municipality, government department or public representative.

🏛️ Government Department Relevant department or office depending on the issue.
🏙️ Municipality / Local Body Local civic issues such as waste, roads, water and public infrastructure.
👥 Public Representatives For appropriate public-interest matters, information may be prepared for discussion with relevant representatives.

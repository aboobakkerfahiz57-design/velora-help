FIRST STEPS

What should you do while waiting?

📍 Give your exact location

Tell the emergency service your location as clearly as possible. Mention nearby landmarks, road names or other useful directions.

📞 Keep your phone available

Keep your phone available for calls from emergency responders. Follow the instructions given by the emergency operator.

🚫 Do not create additional danger

Move away from unsafe areas when possible. Do not enter fire, electrical hazards, traffic hazards or other dangerous areas unless trained and instructed to do so.

🧑‍⚕️ Follow professional instructions

Emergency operators, police officers, firefighters and healthcare professionals are the appropriate people to provide emergency instructions.

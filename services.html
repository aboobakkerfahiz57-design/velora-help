HOW IT WORKS

Simple direction for local services.

1. Tell us what you need

Select the service, location and urgency. You can also send additional details to the VELORA HELP helpdesk.

2. We understand your requirement

Our team can review the enquiry and identify suitable service-provider information available through the platform.

3. Get the direction

Where a suitable verified provider is available, the helpdesk can guide you toward the relevant service.

4. Build a trusted network

As VELORA HELP grows, verified local professionals can join and build service availability across towns and taluks.

About VELORA HELP

One place to find the right direction.

VELORA HELP is designed as a people-focused platform that connects residents with useful information, services, opportunities and appropriate next steps.

🤝 People First

We start with the person's problem instead of expecting them to know which organisation or service to contact.

📍 Local First

The initial focus is Dakshina Kannada and its nine taluks, with the platform designed for future expansion.

🔎 Verified Direction

Official information, responsible referrals and transparent guidance are central to the platform.

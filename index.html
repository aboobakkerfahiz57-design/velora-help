INFORMATION

One place to find the right direction.

VELORA HELP is a people-first guidance platform designed to make it easier for people to explain their problem and find the appropriate next step. We start with Dakshina Kannada and can grow district by district.



🤝 People First

You don't need to know which department, office, hospital, organisation or service you need. Tell VELORA HELP the problem in your own words.

📍 Local First

Our first service area is Dakshina Kannada. The platform is designed to understand the location of the person and guide them toward relevant local options.

🔎 Information First

We aim to provide useful direction, official sources, verified listings and clear next steps rather than simply giving people a long list of websites.

How VELORA HELP works

  • 1. You tell us what you need.
  • 2. You provide your location and basic details.
  • 3. VELORA reviews the request.
  • 4. We identify the appropriate direction.
  • 5. We share relevant information or contacts.
  • 6. You decide the next step.
  • 7. Genuine community problems can be tracked as the platform grows.

⚠️ Important responsibility

VELORA HELP is not a government department, hospital, doctor, police service or emergency authority. VELORA HELP provides guidance and connects people with relevant information and services. For medical emergencies, call the appropriate emergency service immediately. Government scheme eligibility should always be confirmed through the official government source.

DIGITAL SAFETY

Stay safe while using online services.

🔐 Never share OTPs

VELORA HELP will never need your bank OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN or password to provide general digital guidance.

🌐 Use official websites

Whenever an official government or institutional portal is required, verify that you are using the genuine website.

💳 Be careful with payments

Before making a payment, verify the recipient, amount and purpose. Do not send money to unknown people based only on a message or phone call.

📱 Protect personal documents

Avoid sharing Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, passwords or other sensitive information unless it is genuinely required by a trusted official service.

HOW VELORA HELP WORKS

A simple direction system.

01 — Tell us

Explain what help you need, where you are located and what kind of support you are looking for.

02 — We receive your enquiry

Your enquiry can be prepared and sent directly to the VELORA HELP WhatsApp helpdesk.

03 — We understand your need

The VELORA HELP team can review the request and identify possible local resources or organisations.

04 — We give direction

Where reliable information is available, we can guide you toward an appropriate next step.

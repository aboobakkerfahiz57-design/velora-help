🚨 Important Emergency Notice

If an elderly person is experiencing an immediate medical emergency, serious injury, fire, crime, or another life-threatening situation, do not wait for a VELORA HELP response.

Contact the appropriate emergency service immediately.

Privacy & Safety

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, bank PINs, UPI PINs or other highly sensitive security information through this enquiry form. For healthcare concerns, VELORA HELP provides information and direction only. Diagnosis, treatment and medical decisions should be handled by qualified healthcare professionals. Always verify the identity and credentials of any third-party service provider before allowing them into your home or sharing personal documents.
